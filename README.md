# Hi there👋 I'm Yaroslav Usenko, but you can call me Yar

> My mission is to turn the chaos of war into technology, exchanging human lives for robotic ones.

I was an assault trooper and combat medic in the [3rd Separate Assault Brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine](https://ab3.army/).  
After receiving a severe injury, I went through a long recovery. Once the critical phase of treatment was over, out of boredom, I opened a drone assembly lab right in the hospital.  

Later, at an incubator by the Kyiv School of Economics, I came up with **Strike Sense** — an acoustic localization system for detecting artillery and drones.  
Why this? Because as an assault trooper and infantryman, I equally disliked artillery — both enemy and “ours”.  

- 🎖️ Veteran of the [3rd Separate Assault Brigade](https://ab3.army/) (assault trooper, combat medic)  
- 🚀 Founder of **Strike Sense**  
- 🛠️ Engineer & inventor (Python, Node.js, React, 3D printing, electronics)
